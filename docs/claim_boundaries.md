# Claim Boundaries

These boundaries apply to the `regcap` package.

## Allowed claims

- Domestic regulatory and balance-sheet constraints are plausible mechanisms shaping who absorbs marginal Treasury supply when foreign absorption is limited.
- SLR/leverage pressure can be treated as a mechanism and regime classifier, with current event evidence mixed and cautionary.
- LCR/HQLA demand may tilt bank demand toward liquid safe assets, but needs stronger measurement or threshold variation for strong claims.
- G-SIB and reporting-date incentives may create temporary capacity windows.
- Dealer inventory accumulation can be interpreted as short-run intermediation or warehousing.
- Duration-loss, AOCI, and HTM pressure are plausible regime-dependent constraints, especially around 2022-23 and QT normalization.

## Not allowed

- Do not claim SLR relief robustly caused Treasury absorption.
- Do not turn H.8 bank-group screens into individual-bank evidence.
- Do not treat dealer inventory accumulation as final domestic absorption.
- Do not claim domestic regulation caused the TDC deposit response.
- Do not duplicate raw Call Report, FR Y-9C, or FR Y-15 ingestion in this package.
- Do not write to sibling projects.

## Required public posture

Use `regcap` outputs as mechanism architecture and design classification unless a later lane adds a stronger identification design. The current package should help decide which domestic-capacity mechanism deserves promotion, not overstate that any one mechanism explains the TDC deposit footprint.
